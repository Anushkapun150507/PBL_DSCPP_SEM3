About UNIGO

UNIGO is a campus guide made by students, for students. College life is full of small problems that quietly eat up time: finding a classroom or lab you have never visited, working out which campus point your bus leaves from, or tracking down something you lost.

Instead of asking around or scrolling through group chats, UNIGO brings these answers together in one simple place. Bus Finder is available now. A campus map, Lost & Found, and student reviews and tips are on the way.

Our aim is to make campus life easier for everyone, especially new students finding their way for the first time.
